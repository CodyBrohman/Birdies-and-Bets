Privacy Policy

Birdies & Bets: Golf Scorecard · Effective 14 September 2026

Birdies & Bets is a golf scorecard app that keeps score and tracks the side games a group agrees to play. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting service. It makes no network requests while you use it.

What stays on your device

The scores, player names, handicap indexes, and course details you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Money

The app does not hold, move, or request money. It shows a tally of points between players and provides no way to pay, deposit, withdraw, or transfer anything. It contains no links to payment services.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
