Common questions

My course isn't listed. Tap Add your course on the Course screen. Enter the name, then the par and stroke index for each hole. Rating and slope are optional; without them everyone plays gross.

How is the course handicap worked out? Course Handicap = Handicap Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (Course Rating − Par), rounded. The Players screen shows the result and which holes each player gets a stroke on, so you can check it against your club's table.

How do I enter a plus handicap? Type it with a leading plus, for example +1.8. Plus handicaps give strokes back starting from the easiest hole.

A player picked up on a hole. Tap Pick up on their row. The hole records no score, which is different from zero. Each game handles it by its own rules; Stableford scores it as zero, Skins excludes the player from that hole, Stroke Play marks the card incomplete.

I entered a wrong score three holes ago. Open the Card tab and tap the hole number in the header. That takes you back to that hole. Every game recalculates from the full card, so the standings correct themselves.

The app was closed mid-round. Reopen it. The round is saved on every tap and Home offers to resume where you left off.

Can I add a game after we've started? Yes. Games tab → Change games. A new game scores from hole 1 using the scores already on the card. Games that need extra answers per hole, like Wolf, will show those holes as waiting until you fill them in.

Does the app pay people? No. It shows who owes whom in points and stops there. It never holds, moves, or requests money.

Does it need a signal? No. Once you're past the Course screen the whole round works in airplane mode.
